Frequently Asked Questions

Nutmeg and Mace come from the same fruit (Myristica fragrans). Nutmeg is the hard seed inside the fruit, while Mace is the lacy, reddish-orange outer covering (aril) that surrounds the seed. Both have similar flavor profiles but Mace is more delicate and slightly sweeter, while Nutmeg is more robust and earthy.
We supply Ceylon Nutmeg and Mace in three main forms: whole nutmeg seeds, whole mace blades, and powdered versions of both. All forms are available in various packaging sizes from retail packs to bulk containers, with options for private labeling.
