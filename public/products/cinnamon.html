Product Range

Our Cinnamon Forms

Essential Oil

Ceylon Cinnamon Oil

Extracted via steam distillation from the bark of Cinnamomum zeylanicum, our Ceylon Cinnamon Oil is a potent essential oil with powerful antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties.

Its warm, spicy aroma makes it ideal for natural perfumery, therapeutic massage, and holistic wellness blends. Compared to Cassia oils, it has a softer aroma and significantly lower coumarin levels, making it safer for long-term use.

Available in food-grade and cosmetic-grade quality.

Fine Powder

Ceylon Cinnamon Powder

Our finely milled Ceylon Cinnamon Powder is made from high-grade quills including Alba, C4, and C5 varieties. With a naturally sweet, citrusy taste and delicate aroma, it's favored by gourmet chefs and food manufacturers.

Unlike Cassia-based powders that can contain up to 5% coumarin, Ceylon cinnamon has <0.004% coumarin, making it the preferred choice for health-conscious consumers. It dissolves easily in warm liquids.

Sourced from sustainably managed plantations in Sri Lanka.

Quill Sticks

Ceylon Cinnamon Sticks (Quills)

Hand-rolled into delicate quills, our Ceylon Cinnamon Sticks are available in premium grades such as Alba (the thinnest and most refined), C4, and C5. These sticks are highly aromatic, easy to grind, and widely used in beverages and desserts.

The thin, brittle texture of Ceylon quills contrasts starkly with the thick, barky Cassia sticks. Our sticks are sun-dried, manually sorted, and tightly packed to retain flavor. Popular with tea producers and artisanal food brands.

Each stick is a symbol of Sri Lanka's cinnamon craftsmanship.
