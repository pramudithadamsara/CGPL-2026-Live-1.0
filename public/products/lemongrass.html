Premium Ceylon Lemongrass
from Sri Lanka's Highlands

Ceylon Lemongrass (Cymbopogon citratus) is one of Sri Lanka's most aromatic exports. Known for its citrusy fragrance and therapeutic versatility, we offer lemongrass in three export-grade formats: fresh stalks, dried leaves, and essential oil. Naturally grown, hand-harvested, and processed to retain full potency.

Our lemongrass is cultivated in the highland and lowland zones of Sri Lanka without synthetic pesticides. It contains high levels of citral, the key bioactive responsible for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and aromatic properties.

Our Lemongrass Forms

Fresh Stalks

Fresh Lemongrass Stalks

Our fresh lemongrass is harvested in the early morning to preserve essential oil content. It is quickly cleaned and packed in moisture-retaining, food-grade export cartons. Ideal for culinary use in curries, broths, soups, and marinades.

Export buyers love the long shelf life (10–15 days chilled) and oil-rich inner cores that outperform Southeast Asian grades in both aroma and taste.

Available in whole stalks or custom-cut packs (MOQ applies).

Dried Leaves

Dried Lemongrass Leaves

Our dried lemongrass is naturally sun-dried and carefully hand-cut to retain volatile compounds. Suitable for herbal teas, spice blends, potpourri, and bath infusions, our cut leaves have a golden-green appearance, strong citral aroma, and < 10% moisture.

Ideal for brands looking to create lemongrass-based detox teas, wellness infusions, or flavoring extracts. We offer cut & sifted (C/S), whole dried, or powdered lemongrass in food-grade or pharmaceutical-grade standards.

Custom mesh size and bulk packaging available.

Essential Oil

Lemongrass Essential Oil

Our steam-distilled lemongrass oil is rich in citral (75–85%), making it highly valued for aromatherapy, insect repellent products, massage oils, and natural cosmetics. It's clear yellow, aromatic, and free from synthetic solvents or additives.

Popular in diffuser blends, skin toners, pain balms, and natural cleansers, our lemongrass oil is tested for microbial safety and GC-MS profile.

Available in 30ml, 100ml, 1L, and 25L containers.
