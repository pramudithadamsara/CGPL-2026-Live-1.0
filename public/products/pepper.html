Product Range

Our Pepper Forms

Black Pepper

Ceylon Black Pepper

Our Black Pepper is harvested when nearly mature and sun-dried until the outer husk wrinkles into its classic deep brown-black tone. Its robust heat and citrus finish make it ideal for spice blends, sauces, marinades, and essential oil distillation.

Rich in piperine, this variety helps enhance nutrient absorption and is popular in Ayurvedic and wellness formulations. Unlike mass-market pepper, Ceylon Black Pepper retains its natural essential oil through gentle processing.

Whole · Cracked · Ground formats available.

White Pepper

Ceylon White Pepper

White Pepper is made by soaking fully ripened pepper berries and removing the dark skin. This exposes the creamy core with a refined, earthy flavor—ideal for light sauces, soups, and European cuisine where color matters.

Sri Lankan White Pepper has less bitterness and more aroma compared to counterparts from Southeast Asia. It's also used in functional beverages, digestive blends, and natural medicine.

Naturally decorticated and sun-dried for purity.
