About Ceylonspizee

Welcome to Ceylonspizee

Transparency. Traceability. Reliability.

Ceylonspizee Global (Pvt) Ltd is a certified organic Ceylon cinnamon exporter and bulk spice supplier based in Sri Lanka, sourcing directly from verified smallholder farmer networks. We supply wholesale Ceylon cinnamon (Cinnamomum verum), piperine-rich black pepper, high-curcumin organic turmeric, and rare Walla Patta Agarwood Oud oil.

Designed for organic food brands, spice importers, private label spice buyers, and luxury fragrance houses sourcing pure Sri Lankan Oud oil, Ceylonspizee delivers a fully traceable, ethical supply chain certified to international standards. Our proprietary B2B digital traceability portal automates region-specific import documentation for the USA, EU, and UAE, ensuring error-free customs clearance, faster lead times, and complete farm-to-delivery transparency. We especially welcome F&B startups and emerging spice brands worldwide looking to source premium Ceylon cinnamon and organic Ceylon spices without the barrier of large minimum order quantities — our low MOQ, flexible pricing model is built specifically to help early-stage businesses access farm-direct true cinnamon and rare Sri Lankan spices, compete with established brands, and scale confidently. Whether you need bulk true cinnamon, wholesale black pepper, organic turmeric powder, or rare agarwood — we connect your business directly to Sri Lanka's finest certified organic harvests.
