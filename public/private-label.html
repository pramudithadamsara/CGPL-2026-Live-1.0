Industry Basics

What Is Private Label Cinnamon? (And Why It's a Winning Business Model)

Private label means you sell a product — like Ceylon cinnamon — under your own brand name and packaging, while we handle the sourcing, processing, and manufacturing behind the scenes.

You don't need a farm, a factory, or any food production knowledge. You provide the brand vision; we supply the world-class product inside the bottle.

It's how thousands of successful spice, wellness, and food brands operate globally — and it's one of the fastest ways to launch a premium food product with low startup risk.

What Your Label Must Include (Legal Requirements)

  • • Product name (e.g., "Ceylon Cinnamon" or "Cinnamomum verum")
  • • Net weight (in grams/oz depending on market)
  • • Country of origin ("Product of Sri Lanka")
  • • Ingredient list (single ingredient for pure spices)
  • • Allergen information (spices are typically allergen-free, but must state if processed in shared facilities)
  • • Best before / expiry date
  • • Storage instructions
  • • Distributor / importer address
  • • Barcode / EAN (for retail)

Clean Label

What Is "Clean Label" and Why Does It Matter for Your Brand?

Clean label is one of the fastest-growing trends in food and wellness. Consumers are reading ingredient lists — and rejecting products with additives, fillers, artificial colours, or preservatives.

A clean label product is exactly what it says: simple, honest ingredients with nothing hidden. For spices, this means 100% pure spice, single-origin, with no anti-caking agents, no silicon dioxide fillers, no artificial flavour enhancers.

Ceylon cinnamon and our full spice range are naturally clean label — zero additives, traceable from a named farm in Sri Lanka to your consumer's table. This is a powerful commercial differentiator your brand can own.

🏷️ The Clean Label Checklist for Spices:

✓ Single ingredient (100% spice)
✓ No artificial additives
✓ No anti-caking agents
✓ No silicon dioxide (SiO₂)
✓ No irradiation (or disclosed)
✓ No GMO inputs
✓ Traceable origin
✓ Third-party lab verified

FAQ

Private Label Spice — Common Questions Answered

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for private label cinnamon? expand_more
Our MOQs are designed to be startup-friendly. For sample orders, we supply small quantities with no production commitment. For private label production, MOQs typically start from LCL (Less than Container Load) — roughly 500kg–2 tonnes depending on the spice and packaging format. Full container (FCL) orders are available for higher volumes. Contact us with your target quantity and we'll propose the best options.
Do I need food industry experience to start a private label spice brand? expand_more
Not at all. Many of our most successful brand partners launched with no food industry background. We guide you through every step: product selection, grade choices, packaging options, label compliance requirements for your market, import procedures, and logistics. Your job is to build your brand and reach your customers — we handle the production expertise.
What is the difference between Ceylon cinnamon grades (C4, C5, H1, Hamburg, Mexican)? expand_more
Ceylon cinnamon is graded primarily by quill length, uniformity, and number of compound quills per bundle. C4 and C5 are premium grades — long, tightly rolled, uniform quills ideal for premium retail display. H1 and H2 are slightly shorter. Hamburg is a mid-grade with good appearance for retail. Mexican is the most cost-effective grade — still genuine Ceylon cinnamon, used widely in the US market. We provide samples of all grades so you can choose the right visual quality for your brand positioning.
How long does it take from enquiry to first delivery? expand_more
For new clients, the typical timeline from first enquiry to delivery is 6–10 weeks: 1–2 weeks for sampling and quotation, 1–2 weeks for packaging and label approval, 2–3 weeks for production and QC, and 2–4 weeks sea freight transit depending on your destination. Reorders are significantly faster once your specifications and artwork are finalised.
Can I sell private label Ceylon cinnamon on Amazon? expand_more
Yes. Private label Ceylon cinnamon is an excellent Amazon product category — it's searchable, consumable, and differentiable from generic cinnamon at a premium price point. We can help you choose Amazon-appropriate packaging formats (pouches and glass jars work best), ensure label compliance for your target Amazon marketplace (US, UK, EU, etc.), and supply in FBA-ready quantities.
Can I get organic certified private label spices? expand_more
Yes. We supply USDA and EU organic certified Ceylon cinnamon, black pepper, cardamom, cloves, and turmeric. Organic certification adds a measurable retail price premium and is highly sought after by wellness and natural food brands. Certified product quantities are limited — please enquire early if organic is a requirement for your brand.
What documents do I need to import spices into the US, EU, or UK? expand_more
We prepare all standard export documents: commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate, and Certificate of Analysis (CoA). For the US market, FDA Prior Notice filing is required. For the EU, an EU Entry Document and specific pesticide residue compliance documentation may be needed. For the UK, standard phytosanitary and CoA documentation applies. Your export manager will advise on all requirements for your specific destination.
Can you develop a custom spice blend for my brand? expand_more
Absolutely. Our food technology team develops bespoke spice blends and seasoning mixes to your exact flavour brief — whether it's a signature chai spice blend, curry powder range, cinnamon sugar mix, or functional wellness blend. You own the recipe IP once agreed. We supply samples for your approval before proceeding to any production run.
