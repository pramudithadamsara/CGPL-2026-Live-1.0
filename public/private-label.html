What Is Private Label?
(And Why It Wins)

Private label means you sell a premium product — like true Ceylon cinnamon — under your own brand, while we handle the complex sourcing, processing, and manufacturing behind the scenes.

You don't need a farm, a factory, or deep food production knowledge. You provide the brand vision and marketing; we supply the world-class, export-grade product inside the bottle.

It's how thousands of successful wellness, spice, and lifestyle brands operate globally. It is the fastest, lowest-risk path to launching a high-margin food product.

Clean Label

What Is "Clean Label"
and Why Does It Matter?

Clean label is one of the fastest-growing trends in food and wellness. Consumers are actively reading ingredient lists — and rejecting products with additives, fillers, artificial colours, or preservatives.

A clean label product is exactly what it says: simple, honest ingredients with nothing hidden. For spices, this means 100% pure spice, single-origin, with no anti-caking agents, no silicon dioxide fillers, and no artificial flavour enhancers.

Ceylon cinnamon and our full spice range are naturally clean label — zero additives, fully traceable from a named farm in Sri Lanka directly to your consumer's table. This is a powerful commercial differentiator.

🏷️

The Clean Label Checklist for Spices

✓ Single ingredient (100% spice)
✓ No artificial additives

✓ No GMO inputs
✓ Traceable origin
