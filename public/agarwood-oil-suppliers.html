What is Agarwood Oil?

Ceylon Agarwood Oil (Oud Oil): The World's Most Precious Fragrance Ingredient

Agarwood oil — also known as oud oil, oudh oil, or Dihn al Oudh in Arabic — is a rare and extraordinarily valuable essential oil extracted from the resin-saturated heartwood of Aquilaria and Gyrinops trees. When these trees become infected by a specific mold (Phialophora parasitica), they produce a dark, aromatic resin as a defense mechanism. This resin-impregnated wood — called agarwood or oud — is then steam- or hydro-distilled to produce pure agarwood oil.

Ceylon agarwood oil, specifically from Gyrinops walla (locally known as Walla Patta), is Sri Lanka's unique contribution to the global oud oil market. It is prized for a smoother, less animalic, and more refined fragrance profile compared to Southeast Asian varieties — making it highly desirable for niche perfumery and luxury blending.

Often referred to as "liquid gold," genuine agarwood oil is among the world's most expensive natural ingredients by weight — rivaling gold and saffron in value.
