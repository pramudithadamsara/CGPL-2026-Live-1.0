Ceylon Cinnamon Quills

Ceylon Cinnamon quills are hand-rolled cylinders of peeled inner bark, dried to produce the characteristic multi-layered, thin, papery rolls unique to Cinnamomum verum. Unlike thick single-layered Cassia sticks, Ceylon quills are tightly nested, delicate, and easily powdered by hand.

Our quills are graded strictly to Sri Lanka Standard (SLS 81) and internationally accepted specifications. Each consignment is lot-tested for moisture, volatile oil content, coumarin, and microbial limits.

GI Protected Origin

Ceylon Cinnamon:
A Protected
Geographical Indication

Ceylon Cinnamon holds one of the world's most important agricultural Geographical Indication (GI) registrations — a legal certification that this spice originates exclusively from Sri Lanka and carries the qualities associated with its terroir, traditional knowledge, and unique biology.

The GI is registered under Sri Lanka's Intellectual Property Act No. 36 of 2003 and is internationally recognised, providing legal protection against misrepresentation in export markets.

Importer's Guide

How to Import Ceylon Cinnamon:
EU & USA Step-by-Step

Importing Ceylon Cinnamon is straightforward with the right documentation. As your supplier, we prepare and assist with all export documentation. Below is the complete import process for the two largest cinnamon import markets.

Importing to the EU

European Union — All 27 Member States

1

Phytosanitary Certificate

Issued by Sri Lanka's Department of Agriculture. Mandatory for all spice imports into the EU under Regulation (EU) 2016/2031.

2

Certificate of Origin (GSP)

Form A / EUR.1 from Sri Lanka EDB for Generalised System of Preferences duty reduction. Sri Lanka has GSP+ status — 0% import duty on cinnamon.

3

Customs Entry — CN Code

Whole/broken quills: CN 0906 11 00. Ground/powder: CN 0906 20 00. File through your EU customs broker or ATLAS/CHIEF system.

4

Food Safety & Contaminant Compliance

EU Regulation 1334/2008 on flavourings and coumarin limits. Ceylon Cinnamon is explicitly exempted from coumarin restrictions — unlike Cassia.

5

Organic Import (Optional)

EU Organic imports require Control Body Certificate (e.g., IMO, BCS, ECOCERT) and declaration under EU Regulation 2018/848.

Importing to the USA

United States — FDA & USDA regulated

1

FDA Prior Notice

All food shipments to the USA require FDA Prior Notice submission via PNSI system at least 2 hours (air) or 8 hours (ocean) before arrival.

2

USDA/APHIS Phytosanitary Certificate

Sri Lanka issued Phytosanitary Certificate required for plant product clearance at US port of entry under USDA APHIS regulations.

3

US Customs Entry — HTS Code

Whole cinnamon (quills): HTS 0906.11.0000. Ground cinnamon (powder): HTS 0906.20.0000. File CBP Entry 3461/7501 through licensed customs broker.

4

FDA FSMA Compliance

Foreign Supplier Verification Program (FSVP) applies. Your US importer must conduct supplier verification. We provide full FSVP documentation support.

5

USDA Organic (Optional)

USDA NOP certified organic cinnamon available. Requires NOP Organic Certificate from accredited certifier. We supply USDA NOP certified stock.

Alba and C5 Special are the highest-value grades preferred by specialty food, pharmaceutical, and luxury retail sectors.
