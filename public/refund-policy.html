Returns

We accept returns within 7 days from the date of delivery. To be eligible for a return, your product must be unused, in its original condition, and in its original packaging.

Refunds

Once we receive your returned item and inspect it, we will notify you regarding the status of your refund. Approved refunds will be processed to your original payment method. Please note that shipping charges incurred during delivery are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error.

Exchanges

If you wish to exchange a product for a different item, please contact our customer support within 7 days of receiving your order. We will guide you on the exchange process.

Non-Returnable Items

Certain products cannot be returned or refunded, including:

  • Perishable goods (spices, fresh products)
  • Customized or personalized orders
  • Products damaged due to improper handling

Damaged or Defective Items

If your order arrives damaged or defective, contact us immediately. We will provide a replacement or refund, depending on your preference and product availability.

You are responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is due to an error on our side (wrong or defective product). In such cases, we will provide a prepaid shipping label.
