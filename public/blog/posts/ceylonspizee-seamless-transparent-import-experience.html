Process

Simplifying the Import Process for Global Buyers

While CeylonSpizee is transforming the lives of Sri Lankan spice farmers, we're also making the import process dramatically easier and smarter for our international B2B buyers.

Through the CeylonSpizee Client Portal, we've built a fully digital, centralized system that removes nearly every traditional bottleneck faced by spice importers:

  • No more endless back-and-forth emails
  • Instant access to documentation (commercial invoice, packing list, delivery notes)
  • Live order tracking from harvest to shipment
  • Clear visibility into product quality, source location, and timelines
  • Reliable communication and faster response times

From the moment you place an order to the moment it lands at your port — every step is traceable, transparent, and trackable in real time.

Transparency

Full Traceability: From Farm to Fork

We've designed our system so that every batch of spice you import from CeylonSpizee carries its full origin story.

  • Know which farmer grew your cinnamon
  • See which village your turmeric came from
  • Know when it was harvested
  • Track how it was processed and packed
  • Monitor how it's moving through the supply chain

This level of traceability means that even the end consumer eating a product made from CeylonSpizee spices can scan or trace back the origin — down to the exact farmer who cultivated it.
