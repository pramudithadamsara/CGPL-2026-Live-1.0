Overview

What is Agarwood Oil and Why is it So Valuable?

Agarwood Oil, often called "liquid gold," is one of the most expensive natural oils in the world. Extracted from Aquilaria trees, the oil forms when the tree becomes infected by a specific type of mold, prompting a protective resin.

Sri Lanka is emerging as a sustainable source of this rare oil, known for its warm, woody, and complex aroma. It is widely used in luxury perfumery, traditional medicine, and spiritual ceremonies across Asia and the Middle East. A single kilogram of top-grade Agarwood Oil can fetch over $30,000 in the global market.
