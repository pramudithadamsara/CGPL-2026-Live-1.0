European Union

EU Regulatory Updates

The European Union has implemented stricter requirements for organic spice imports under Regulation (EU) 2018/848. Importers must now provide detailed traceability documentation for each shipment.

These new regulations aim to enhance transparency and ensure that organic products maintain their integrity throughout the supply chain. The requirements include detailed record-keeping, certification verification, and batch-level traceability.

North America

North American Requirements

FDA's Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA) requires foreign suppliers to comply with the Foreign Supplier Verification Program (FSVP). This includes comprehensive documentation of food safety practices.

The FSVP mandates that importers verify their foreign suppliers are implementing preventive controls that meet US food safety standards. This requires thorough documentation and ongoing verification processes.

Standards

Labeling Standards

New labeling requirements mandate clear indication of origin, organic certification status, and allergen declarations. Non-compliance can result in shipment rejection and significant penalties.

Accurate labeling is critical for regulatory compliance and consumer protection. Importers must ensure all product labels meet the specific requirements of each destination market, including language specifications and format requirements.
